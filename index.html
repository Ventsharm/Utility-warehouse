What is Utility Warehouse?

UW is a UK multiservice provider. Instead of dealing with lots of separate companies, you can choose from several everyday household services and manage eligible services together.

⚡

Energy

UW says customers taking a variable energy service plus two other eligible bundle services can save around £48 a year on average compared with standard variable tariffs from major suppliers, based on typical usage. Your actual saving will depend on your home, usage and tariff.

📶

Broadband

UW says switching to its Full Fibre 150 broadband after your current contract has ended could save around £44 a year, based on its comparison with similar broadband from major providers over three years.

📱

Mobile

UW currently says its Unlimited Max plan can save £182 over 12 months compared with equivalent rolling unlimited-data tariffs from several major networks. It also offers lower-cost mobile options, so you can choose what suits your usage.

🛡️

Insurance

UW also offers household insurance products, helping you keep more of your essential services together. Prices and potential savings depend on the cover you need and your individual quote.

How can UW help you save?

UW is designed around bringing household services together. The more eligible services you bundle, the more savings and benefits you may be able to unlock. Current UW comparisons show potential savings across energy, broadband and mobile, although the amount you personally save will depend on the services, tariffs and deals you are comparing.

Save more with the UW Cashback Card

The Cashback Card is another way UW customers can reduce their household bill simply by using the card for everyday spending.

💳

Top up and spend

Load money onto your Cashback Card from your bank account or debit card using the UW app, then use it online or in shops. You can also add it to Apple Pay or Google Pay.

🛍️

Earn cashback

Earn up to 10% cashback with participating UW retail partners. Eligible spending at other retailers can earn 1% cashback, subject to monthly limits and terms.

🧾

Lower your UW bill

Your earned cashback is added as credit to your UW bill, so ordinary spending such as shopping can help reduce what you pay for your UW services.

Cashback Card: UW currently charges £3 per month after any applicable free trial. Cashback rates, participating retailers, eligibility, limits and offers can change, so check the latest UW terms when joining.
