Become a Utility Warehouse Partner.

UW Partners introduce people to Utility Warehouse and can earn commission when eligible customers sign up. It can be done flexibly alongside other commitments, with training, online resources, tools and Partner support available.

What does a UW Partner do?

Partners help people understand UW’s household services and refer customers who decide the services are right for them. Partners can also introduce other people to the Partner opportunity.

🤝

Help people

Introduce people to UW and help them explore services that may suit their household.

💷

Earn commission

UW currently offers upfront and ongoing commission opportunities for qualifying customer referrals. Earnings vary and are not guaranteed.

🕒

Work flexibly

Build your Partner activity around your own schedule, whether you treat it as extra income or develop it further.

Support to help you get started

UW says new Partners receive introductory training, online resources and support. You can use the Partner Portal and Partner app as part of your day-to-day Partner toolkit.

Earnings note: Partner income depends on activity, eligible customer sign-ups and UW’s current commission plan. It should not be treated as guaranteed income.
